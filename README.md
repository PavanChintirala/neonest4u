# Neonest

Create a modern SaaS landing page for a startup called NeoHome. NeoHome is an AI-powered platform designed for apartment communities, HOAs, and property management companies. The platform provides 24/7 AI assistance for residential communities through two main experiences: an AI phone agent for property managers and an AI chatbot for residents. The landing page should feel radiant, modern, classy, and premium, similar to high-end AI startups. It should be visually engaging, clean, and easy to understand within seconds. Use soft glowing gradients, modern typography, rounded UI components, and subtle animations to create a futuristic yet professional look. Color palette: • electric blue • soft violet • gradient glow accents • clean white background The design should feel like a next-generation AI product. ⸻ Hero Section Headline: Your 24/7 AI Front Desk for Modern Communities Subheadline: NeoHome helps property managers reduce operational workload while giving residents instant answers and support through AI-powered voice and chat assistants. Primary CTA buttons: Request Demo Join Early Access Hero visual idea: Illustration or graphic showing an AI assistant handling resident calls and chats while organizing community requests. ⸻ Problem Section Explain the challenges faced by property managers and communities: • Constant resident requests and questions • Manual tracking of maintenance issues • Slow response times • Fragmented communication between residents and management Use clean icons to represent each problem. ⸻ Solution Section Introduce NeoHome as the solution. Explain that NeoHome provides a 24/7 AI layer for community operations, handling resident interactions automatically and organizing requests for property management teams. ⸻ Features Section Create three visually distinct feature cards. AI Phone Agent for Property Managers The AI answers incoming resident calls and understands their requests. It logs issues, answers common questions, and creates structured tickets automatically, reducing the workload for management teams. Resident AI Chat Assistant Residents can use a simple AI chatbot to ask questions about their community. They can check rules, amenities, maintenance updates, and get instant answers anytime. Smart Maintenance Coordination When a resident reports an issue, NeoHome automatically categorizes the request and creates an organized workflow for the property management team. ⸻ How It Works Show a simple 4-step process: Resident calls or chats AI understands the request Ticket or request created Management team receives organized information Use simple icons and connecting lines to show the flow. ⸻ Benefits Section Highlight benefits clearly: Reduce operational workload Improve response time for residents Provide 24/7 community support Keep maintenance requests organized ⸻ Who It’s For Show three customer groups: Apartment Communities HOA Communities Property Management Companies Each with a short description. ⸻ Vision Section Explain the long-term vision: NeoHome will evolve into an AI operating system for residential communities, automating communication, maintenance coordination, and resident support. ⸻ Final Call to Action Headline: Bring AI to Your Community CTA buttons: Request Demo Join the Waitlist ⸻ Footer Include: NeoHome logo Contact email placeholder Social media placeholders Copyright information
